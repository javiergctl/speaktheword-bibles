Privacy Policy

SpeakTheWord · com.prevision.speaktheword · Last updated 16 September 2026

SpeakTheWord does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you write, record, or read in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in SpeakTheWord is stored only on your phone or tablet:

  • Your proclamation lists, the passages in them, and your personal notes
  • Voice recordings you make, if you record yourself into a proclamation
  • Your settings — voice, speed, theme, text size, chosen translations
  • Your reading position and bookmarks
  • Your schedules and reminders

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to us, because there is nowhere for it to go — the app has no server that holds user data and no account to attach it to. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

The microphone

SpeakTheWord can record your own voice so you can place it inside a proclamation alongside Scripture. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you use that feature, and you can decline — every other part of the app works without it.

Recordings are written to the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded. They are not analysed, not transcribed, and not sent to any speech service. You can delete any recording from within the app.

What the app downloads

SpeakTheWord works offline. It connects to the internet only to fetch content you ask for, and these are downloads — the app sends no personal information in the process:

  • Bible texts and the Strong's lexicon, from GitHub, when you choose to download a translation or the original-language study pack
  • Neural voice models, from Hugging Face, if you choose a higher-quality voice
  • Curated proclamation lists and background audio, from cdn.speaktheword.app

As with any request to any website, the servers that host these files (GitHub, Hugging Face, and our own content host) may record the request in their ordinary server logs, which typically include an IP address. We do not use those logs to identify or profile anyone, and we do not combine them with anything else.

Text to speech

Spoken playback uses the text-to-speech engine built into your device, or a neural voice model downloaded to your device and run there. In both cases the speech is produced on the device itself. Verse text is not sent to a cloud speech service.

Notifications

If you schedule a proclamation, SpeakTheWord uses your device's local notifications to remind you. These are scheduled on the device. No notification passes through a server of ours, and nothing about your schedule leaves the phone.

Children

SpeakTheWord is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — of any age — because we do not collect personal information at all.

Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data, because we do not hold any. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Your control

  • Decline or revoke microphone and notification permissions in your device settings at any time
  • Delete individual lists, notes, and recordings inside the app
  • Remove downloaded Bibles and the study pack from within the app to reclaim space
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it has stored

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If a future version of the app ever collects personal information — for instance if accounts or cloud backup are added — this page will say so plainly, and the app will ask for your consent before any such feature is used.
